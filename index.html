Research

Creep of Grade 91 steel

M.Sc. by research at Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah (UMPSA), Faculty of Mechanical and Automotive Engineering Technology, supervised by Dr. Nasrul Azuan Alang. I hold the Malaysia International Scholarship for 2026/2027.

I'm predicting uniaxial creep behaviour and rupture life of P91 steel. The Omega creep model runs in Abaqus through a user-defined creep subroutine, and I compare its predictions with experimental creep data.

Publications

  • A Continuum Damage Coupled Unified Constitutive Model for Creep-Fatigue Damage Evaluation of Modified Grade 91 Tube Sheet Structures Under Flexible Loading ConditionsIslam, N. & Hossain, M. S. ASME PVP 2024, Bellevue, Washington. doi:10.1115/PVP2024-122915
  • Creep-Fatigue Life Evaluation of Modified Grade 91 Tube Sheet Structures Under Flexible Loading ConditionsIslam, N. & Hossain, M. S. ASME PVP 2023, Atlanta, Georgia. doi:10.1115/PVP2023-106617

LoadPath

ETABS plugins and design tools I build in C#.

Most structural checks are the same steps every project. LoadPath turns them into ETABS plugins. The first is the BNBC Irregularity Checker, which checks building irregularities and P-delta stability to BNBC 2020.
